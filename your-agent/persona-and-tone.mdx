---
title: "Customizing your agent"
description: "How to shape how your agent looks and presents itself to visitors."
---

Open **Customize** in your dashboard. A live preview shows your changes as you make them, and Try agent floats the real agent over your dashboard so you can test a conversation.

## Brand

Set your primary and secondary colours and your business name.

## Position and theme

Choose where your agent floats on your site (left, center or right) and whether it looks light or dark.

## Your agent's persona

Give your agent a name and an avatar. Visitors see them on the agent's first message. The name is separate from your organisation's name, and shows as "Agent name from Organisation name".

You can also change the placeholder text in the box where visitors type. Use `{agent_name}` to insert your agent's name, or leave it empty to use the standard wording.

## Suggested questions

Add up to three questions that appear as chips when someone opens your agent. Leave a box empty to hide it.

If your plan includes the [Estimate builder](/addons/estimate-builder) and it is turned on, the estimate prompt appears first. You can edit its label or hide it.
