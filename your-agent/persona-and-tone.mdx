## Your agent has a voice

Lark doesn't sound like a generic chatbot. You define how it introduces itself, how it speaks, and what personality it brings to conversations.

## Agent name

Give your agent a name that fits your brand — your business name, a friendly persona, or something product-focused.

## Greeting message

The first thing a visitor sees when they open the chat. Keep it short, warm, and specific to what your business does.

## Tone

Choose from a range of tone settings — professional, friendly, conversational, formal — or describe your own in plain language.
