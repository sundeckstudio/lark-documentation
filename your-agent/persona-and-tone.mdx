---
title: "Personalizing your agent"
description: "How to shape how your agent looks and presents itself to visitors."
---

Open **Personalize** under Agent in your dashboard. A live preview shows your changes as you make them.

## Brand

Set your primary colour. It is used for the links and buttons in a conversation.

## Position and theme

Choose where your agent floats on your site (left, center or right) and whether it looks light or dark.

## Personalize your agent

Give your agent a name and an avatar. Visitors see them on the agent's first message. The name is separate from your organisation's name, and shows as "Agent name from Organisation name".

You can also change the placeholder text in the box where visitors type. Use `{agent_name}` to insert your agent's name, or leave it empty to use the standard wording.

On Pro and above you can turn on **Hide Lark branding** to remove the "Powered by Lark" footer.

## Suggested questions

Add up to three questions that appear as chips when someone opens your agent. The agent answers them from your [knowledge sources](/your-agent/knowledge-base). Leave a box empty to hide it.

If the [Estimate builder](/addons/estimate-builder) is on, the estimate prompt appears first. You can edit its label or hide it. Even when it is hidden, the agent still runs an estimate when a visitor asks for one.
