## Why the knowledge base matters

Your agent is only as good as the context you give it. Lark doesn't make things up — it answers from what you've added.

## Three ways to add context

### Paste text

Copy and paste your services list, pricing page, FAQs, team bios, process descriptions — anything you'd want a visitor to know.

### Upload a PDF

You can upload up to 5 PDF files (max 25MB each). Lark extracts the content and uses it as part of your knowledge base.

### Add a URL

Paste a URL and Lark will crawl the page and pull in the content automatically.
