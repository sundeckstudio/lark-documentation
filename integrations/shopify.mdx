---
title: "Shopify"
description: "Let your agent look up orders and customer details from your Shopify store."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Shopify.
2. Enter your Shopify store URL and authorise Lark.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Get order by email** — finds a customer's most recent order using their email address.
- **Get order status and tracking** — returns the current status and tracking details for an order.
- **Get customer by email** — retrieves customer account details.
- **Cancel an order** — cancels an open order on the customer's behalf.
- **Flag an order for refund** — marks an order for refund review by your team.

## Identity verification

Before returning any order or customer data, the agent asks the visitor for their email and sends them a one-time verification code. Data is only shared after the code is confirmed.
