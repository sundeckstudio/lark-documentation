---
title: "Slack"
description: "Send lead notifications and messages to a Slack channel."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Slack.
2. Authorise Lark through the Slack OAuth flow and select the workspace.
3. Back in Lark, enter your **Channel ID** in the settings field. This is required before the integration becomes active.

## Finding your Channel ID

In Slack, right-click the channel name, click **View channel details**, and scroll to the bottom. The Channel ID starts with a `C`.

## Actions

- **Post a message to a channel** — sends a message to the configured channel.
- **Send a direct message to a team member** — sends a DM to a specified team member.
- **Post a lead notification to a channel** — sends a formatted notification when a new lead is captured.

<Warning>
  The Channel ID field must be filled before the integration is active. The agent cannot use Slack until this is saved.
</Warning>
