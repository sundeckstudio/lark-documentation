title: "Google Calendar"

---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Google Calendar.
2. Sign in with your Google account and grant Lark calendar access.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Check availability** — finds free slots on your calendar for a given date or range.
- **Book an event** — creates a new calendar event with the visitor's details.
- **Reschedule an event** — moves an existing event to a new time.
- **Cancel an event** — removes an event from the calendar.
- **List upcoming events for a contact** — retrieves upcoming events associated with a contact's email.

## How the booking flow works

When a visitor asks to book, the agent checks availability, presents the next available slots, collects the visitor's name and email, and creates the event. The visitor receives a confirmation in the conversation.
