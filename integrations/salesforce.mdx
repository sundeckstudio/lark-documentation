## What this integration does

The Salesforce integration sends leads captured by your Lark agent directly into Salesforce as a Lead or Contact record.

## What you can do

- Automatically create a Salesforce Lead or Contact when someone submits their details
- Map Lark lead fields to Salesforce fields
- Assign new records to a specific owner or queue
- Include the conversation summary in the Lead description

## Setup

In your Lark dashboard, go to Organisation settings → Integrations. Click Connect next to Salesforce and authorise via OAuth. Configure your field mappings and save.
