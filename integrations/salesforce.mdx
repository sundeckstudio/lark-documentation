---
title: "Salesforce"
description: "Create or update Salesforce Leads from conversations."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Salesforce.
2. Choose **Production** or **Sandbox** for the login.
3. Sign in to Salesforce in the window that opens and approve access.
4. Back in Lark, click **Validate connection**.

## Settings

- **Create or update Lead:** turn on to create a Lead for each new contact, or update the existing one.
- **Lead source:** the source value set on each Lead. The default is `Lark`.

Use **Send a test contact** to check it works, then **Save settings**.
