---
title: "Google Sheets"
description: "Read data from and append rows to a Google Sheet during conversations."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Google Sheets.
2. Sign in with your Google account and grant Lark access.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Look up a row by value** — searches a sheet for a row matching a given value, such as an email or order ID.
- **Get all rows from a sheet** — retrieves all rows, useful for price lists, menus or inventory the agent can reference.
- **Append a new row** — adds a new row to the sheet, for example when a lead is captured.
