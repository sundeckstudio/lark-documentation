## Connect Lark to your stack

Lark integrations let you push leads and conversation data from your agent directly into the tools your team already uses. No manual copying, no missed contacts.

## Available integrations

- **Brevo** — Automatically add contacts captured by your agent to a Brevo list.
- **Mailchimp** — Push new leads straight into a Mailchimp audience.
- **Zendesk** — Create Zendesk tickets from Lark conversations.
- **Salesforce** — Create Lead or Contact records in Salesforce when a lead is captured.
