## Adding an integration

Click **Connect** on the tool's card. Most tools open a sign-in window where you approve access.

A connection is always in one of four states: Not connected, Waiting for approval, Connected or Needs attention. If a tool shows Needs attention, use **Reconnect**.

After connecting, use **Validate connection** to check it works, then adjust the tool's settings and click **Save settings**. Use **Send a test contact** to confirm a contact arrives. **Disconnect** removes the connection.

## Consent from your visitors to add them as a lead

Following data and privacy laws such as GDPR, mailing list tools (Example but not limited to: Brevo and Mailchimp) only receive visitors who said **yes** when the agent asked if they would like to receive emails. Other leads stay in your dashboard in Leads page.

## How syncing works

Leads sync in the background. Each lead in your dashboard shows whether it was sent, is still sending or failed, and you can retry a failed one. In Leads page you can choose the integrations you want to use, to send your leads directly to.

For example if you want to send all leads to Mailchimp, or Salesforce, simply enable the "Where your leads go" function in Leads page and choose the destination. Only connected integrations will appear here.
