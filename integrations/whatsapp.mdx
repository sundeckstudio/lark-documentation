title: "WhatsApp"
description: "Send lead notifications and visitor follow-ups via WhatsApp Business."
---

Available on Pro and above. Requires a WhatsApp Business Account with an approved API key.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on WhatsApp.
2. Enter your WhatsApp Business API key on the secure page that opens.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Send a lead notification** — sends a text message to the business owner's configured WhatsApp number when a new lead is captured.
- **Send a visitor follow-up** — sends a pre-approved message template to the visitor's WhatsApp number after a session ends, if they provided it during the conversation. Common templates include booking confirmations and estimate summaries.
- **Get business phone numbers** — retrieves your registered WhatsApp Business number so the agent can share it with visitors who ask how to reach you directly.

## Before you start

Visitor follow-ups use WhatsApp message templates, which must be approved by Meta before they can be sent. Create and submit your templates in [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/message-templates/) before enabling this action.

<Note>
  The visitor's WhatsApp number must be explicitly provided by them during the conversation. The agent will never infer or guess a phone number.
</Note>
