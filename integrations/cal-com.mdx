title: "Cal.com"
description: "Let your agent check availability and manage bookings in Cal.com."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Cal.com.
2. Enter your Cal.com API key on the secure page that opens.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Check availability** — finds free slots for a given date or range.
- **Book an event** — creates a new booking with the visitor's details.
- **Reschedule an event** — moves an existing booking to a new time.
- **Cancel an event** — cancels an existing booking.
- **List upcoming events for a contact** — retrieves upcoming bookings for a contact's email.
