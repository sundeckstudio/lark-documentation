---
title: "HubSpot"
description: "Create and update HubSpot contacts and deals from conversations."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on HubSpot.
2. Authorise Lark through the HubSpot OAuth flow.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Create a contact** — adds a new contact record when a lead is captured.
- **Update a contact** — updates an existing contact's details.
- **Create a deal** — opens a new deal linked to the contact.
- **Log a note** — adds a note to the contact record.
- **Get contact by email** — looks up an existing contact during a conversation.
