---
title: "Calendly"
description: "Let your agent check availability and share booking links via Calendly."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Calendly.
2. Authorise Lark through the Calendly OAuth flow.
3. Back in Lark, confirm the connection and map your actions.

## Actions

- **Check availability** — finds free slots for a given date or range.
- **Book an event** — generates a scheduling link for the visitor to complete the booking.
- **Reschedule an event** — provides a reschedule link for an existing booking.
- **Cancel an event** — cancels an existing booking.
- **List upcoming events for a contact** — retrieves upcoming events for a contact's email.

<Note>
  Calendly does not support direct booking creation via its API. When a visitor books, the agent generates a personalised scheduling link for them to confirm the time. The booking is completed on Calendly's side.
</Note>
