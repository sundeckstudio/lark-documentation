## What this integration does

The Zendesk integration automatically pushes leads and conversations captured by your Lark agent into Zendesk as support tickets.

## What you'll need

- A Zendesk account with admin access
- Your Zendesk subdomain and API token

## Setup

In your Lark dashboard, go to Organisation settings → Integrations. Click Connect next to Zendesk, enter your subdomain and API token, choose the assignee group, and save.
