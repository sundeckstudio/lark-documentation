---
title: "Zendesk"
description: "Create Zendesk tickets from conversations."
---

Available on Pro and above.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Zendesk.
2. Enter your Zendesk subdomain (the first part of `yourcompany.zendesk.com`).
3. Sign in to Zendesk in the window that opens and approve access.
4. Back in Lark, click **Validate connection**.

## Settings

- **Create tickets:** turn on to create a ticket for each lead.
- **Ticket tag:** the tag added to every ticket Lark creates. The default is `lark`.

Use **Send a test contact** to check it works, then **Save settings**.
