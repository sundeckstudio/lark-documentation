## What this integration does

Visitors who share their contact details with your Lark agent are automatically added as subscribers to a Mailchimp audience of your choice.

## What you'll need

- A Mailchimp account and API key
- At least one Mailchimp audience set up

## Setup

In your Lark dashboard, go to Organisation settings → Integrations. Click Connect next to Mailchimp, enter your API key, select your audience, and save.
