---
title: "Inviting your team"
description: "Bring teammates into your workspace and set what they can do."
---

Go to Settings → Team and enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite.

- Admins manage billing and settings.
- Members work with the day-to-day features.
- Viewers can look but not change anything.

Invitations expire after a few days. You can resend one from the same page.
