## What the rules module does

Rules give you precise control over your agent's behaviour. You can define what topics it should stay focused on, what information it's allowed to share, and what questions it should redirect to your team.

## Examples of rules you might set

- "Do not quote specific prices — direct visitors to book a call instead"
- "Only discuss our web design and branding services"
- "Never share internal team member contact details"
- "Always recommend booking a discovery call for large projects"

## Setting rules

In your Lark dashboard, go to the Rules section and add your rules as plain-language instructions.
