Lark is an AI agent you embed on your website. It answers visitor questions from your own content, can give instant estimates and captures leads for you.

## Where to begin

1. [Set up Lark for the first time](/getting-started/setting-up).
2. Add your business content in [your knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base).
3. [Embed the agent on your website](/getting-started/embedding).
