Lark is an AI agent you add to your website. It answers visitor questions from your own content, gives instant estimates and captures leads for you.

Setup takes about 10 minutes in three steps: shape your agent, teach it your business and add it to your site. Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial.

## Why choose Lark?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Answers from your own content" icon="brain">
    Your agent answers from the text, files and website pages you give it. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to pass the visitor to you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Easy to add" icon="code">
    Paste one script tag into any site that lets you add custom HTML. No plugin to install and no developer needed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lead capture inside the conversation" icon="user-plus">
    No pop-up form. The agent asks for contact details after it has been helpful, confirms the email and records consent separately. Every lead is in your dashboard with a status, notes and CSV export.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Instant estimates" icon="calculator">
    With the Estimate builder add-on, visitors get a scoped price in the chat, without any back and forth.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Connects to your tools" icon="plug">
    Send leads to Brevo, Mailchimp, Zendesk, Salesforce and HubSpot, with more on the way.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Your brand, your look" icon="palette">
    Choose your colour, theme, position, agent name and avatar, and see a live preview as you change them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shape your agent">
    Give your agent a name, an avatar and your brand colour in [Personalize](/your-agent/persona-and-tone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teach it your business">
    Add text, files and website pages in [Your agent](/your-agent/overview). Use Try agent in your dashboard to check the answers before you go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your site">
    Paste one script tag, as shown in [Embedding agent on your website](/getting-started/embedding).
  </Step>
</Steps>
