## Before you start

Every new account starts with a 14-day trial. During the trial you can build your agent and try it inside your dashboard. Putting it live on your website needs an active paid plan.

## Step 1: Build your agent's persona

Answer a few questions about your business and give your agent a name and an avatar. You can change these at any time in **Customize**.

## Step 2: Add your business context

Open **Knowledge** and add the content your agent will answer from: paste text, upload a file or add a page from your website. See [Adding context to your knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base).

## Step 3: Add suggested questions

In **Customize**, add up to three questions that appear as chips when someone opens your agent.

## Step 4: Add it to your site

Open **Add to site**, set your organisation domain and copy the embed code onto your website. See [Embedding agent on your website](/getting-started/embedding).
