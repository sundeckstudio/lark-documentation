## Before you start

You'll need a Lark account on a paid plan. If you're on a trial, you can build and preview your agent but embedding it on a live site requires an active subscription.

## Step 1 — Define your agent

When you first log in, Lark walks you through a short setup to build your agent's persona. You'll set a name for the agent, a greeting, and the tone it should use when talking to your visitors.

## Step 2 — Add your business context

Go to the Knowledge base section and add the content your agent will draw on — your services, pricing, FAQs, process descriptions.

## Step 3 — Set your rules

Use the Rules module to define what your agent should and shouldn't discuss.

## Step 4 — Embed on your site

Copy the embed script from Organisation settings → Embed and paste it into your website before the closing `</body>` tag.
