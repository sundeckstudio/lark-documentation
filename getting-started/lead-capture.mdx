## No lead form required

Lark doesn't use a pop-up form to collect visitor details. Instead, your agent asks for contact information naturally during the conversation — when it makes sense, based on what the visitor is asking about.

## Where leads go

Every contact captured by your agent appears in your Leads list inside the Lark dashboard. You can see the visitor's name, email, what they asked about, and the full conversation.

## Syncing to your tools

Leads can be automatically pushed to your CRM, email platform, or helpdesk via Lark's integrations. See the Integrations section for setup guides.
