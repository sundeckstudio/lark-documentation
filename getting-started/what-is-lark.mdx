## Lark is a multi-contextual AI agent tailored to your business

Lark is multi-contextual, human language, trainable agent that you add to your website in minutes without any technical know-how. It answers questions from your visitors, qualifies leads and hands off to your team, all grounded in your own business content.

Unlike a generic chatbot or hundreds of AI chatbots, Lark does not give generic AI answers. Lark learns from the context you give it: your services, pricing, FAQs and any documents you upload in the training and knowledge sections in the dashboard. It speaks in your brand's voice and only answers accurately from real business knowledge it is trained on, so visitors always get correct, relevant responses.

## What makes Lark different to other AI chatbots

- Lark is multi-contextual, it knows and can gather data from multiple sources such as your CRM, email marketing lists, documentations, websites, articles and most importantly: the data you train it on. This means unlike generic AI chatbots, Lark can take right actions, provide accurate information and takes your customer on a pleasant journey. In most cases, it already has memory of most of your visitors that may be living in one of your systems. All that with a simple, sleek agentic interface on your website.
- Lark is platform-agnostic. It can integrate with hundreds of platforms at once and can intelligently understand data, patterns and information about your business and customers.

## What Lark can do

- **Answer questions:** Lark draws on your knowledge base to respond accurately to visitor questions about your business.
- **Qualify leads:** Lark collects visitor contact details naturally during the conversation and adds them to your leads list.
- **Produce estimates:** Lark walks visitors through a scoped estimate for your services, without any manual back-and-forth.
