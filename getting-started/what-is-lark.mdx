## Lark is an AI agent for your business

Lark is an embeddable AI agent that you add to your website in minutes. It answers questions from your visitors, qualifies leads, and hands off to your team — all grounded in your own business content.

Unlike a generic chatbot, Lark learns from the context you give it: your services, pricing, FAQs, and any documents you upload. It speaks in your brand's voice and only answers what it knows, so visitors always get accurate, relevant responses.

## What Lark can do

- **Answer questions** — Lark draws on your knowledge base to respond accurately to visitor questions about your business.
- **Qualify leads** — Lark collects visitor contact details naturally during the conversation and adds them to your leads list.
- **Produce estimates** — Lark walks visitors through a scoped estimate for your services, without any manual back-and-forth.
- **Enforce boundaries** — Rules let you define exactly what Lark will and won't discuss or share.
