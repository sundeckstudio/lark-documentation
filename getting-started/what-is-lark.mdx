## Lark is a multi-contextual AI agent tailored to your business

Lark is multi-contextual, human language, trainable agent that you **add to your website in minutes** without any technical know-how. It answers questions from your visitors, qualifies leads and hands off to your team, all grounded in your own business content.

It speaks in your brand's voice and only answers accurately from real business knowledge it is trained on, so visitors always get correct, relevant responses.

## What makes Lark different to generic chatbots

### **Lark is multi-contextual**:

Lark can connect and integrate with tools you already use. It knows and can gather data from multiple sources such as your **CRM, email marketing lists, documentations, websites, articles, Stripe data, Help desk** and most importantly: the data you train it on. This means unlike generic AI chatbots, Lark can take right actions, provide accurate information and takes your customer on a pleasant journey. In most cases, it already has memory of most of your visitors that may be living in one of your systems. All that with a simple, sleek conversational interface on your website.

### **Lark is platform-agnostic**

It can integrate with hundreds of platforms at once and can intelligently understand data, patterns and information about your business and customers. It doesn't lock you into use specific CRM or application, you can continue using your existing applications and connect them all into Lark to deliver a modern single conversational interface

### **Communicates like a trained human employee**

Lark is not a scripted chatbot with preset answers. It understands context, asks follow-up questions, and responds the way a knowledgeable team member would. Trained on your business, it speaks in your tone, stays on topic, and handles complex conversations without sounding robotic.

### Learns from feedback

Lark is a generative AI agent and learns from every interaction. The learning and feedback compounds over time, so the longer Lark runs on your business, the sharper it gets.

## What Lark can do

- **Answer questions:** Lark draws on your knowledge base to respond accurately to visitor questions about your business.
- **Qualify leads:** Lark collects visitor contact details naturally during the conversation and adds them to your leads list.
- **Produce estimates:** Lark walks visitors through a scoped estimate for your services, without any manual back-and-forth.
- **Book meetings from a simple conversational interface:** Your visitors will not need to fill another form every again. Lark checks your calendar and offers available slots in the same conversational interface. No forms, no back and forth, just a confirmed meeting.
- **Manage payments and subscriptions for your existing customers:** Connects to Stripe or Shopify and other tools you already use, to answer billing questions, send payment links and handle subscription changes, all inside the same conversational interface, fully powered by AI and trained on your brand context.
