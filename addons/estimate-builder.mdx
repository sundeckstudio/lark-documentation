---
title: "Estimate builder"
description: "Let your agent give visitors an instant, accurate estimate."
---

The Estimate builder is an addon that lets your agent walk a visitor through a few questions and give a priced estimate. It is included in every plan.

## Turn it on

Open **Estimate builder** under Connections & tools and turn it on. It starts blank, so nothing is offered until you set it up.

## Set it up

- **Questions:** what the agent asks the visitor.
- **Pricing table:** your pricing rules. Prices are calculated on the server from these rules, never by the AI.
- **Send via email:** how the estimate is sent to the visitor.

## For visitors

When the Estimate builder is on, a "Get an estimate" chip appears first in your agent. You can edit its label or hide it in [Customize](/your-agent/persona-and-tone).

Each estimate becomes a lead on your dashboard with its price breakdown.
