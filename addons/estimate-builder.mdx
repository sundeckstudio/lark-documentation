## What the estimate builder does

The estimate builder lets visitors request a scoped project estimate directly inside the chat. Your agent asks them a series of structured questions — scope, timeline, requirements — and produces an estimate based on your pricing and service structure. No forms, no back-and-forth emails.

## How it's triggered

The estimate flow is one of your suggested questions — typically something like "Get a project estimate". When a visitor taps it, the agent switches into estimate mode and walks them through your configured questions.

## Setting it up

In your Lark dashboard, go to the Estimate builder settings. Define your questions, set your pricing structure, and configure the output format.
