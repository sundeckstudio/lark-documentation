---
title: "Understanding your analytics"
description: "How each component of your Lark analytics works and what it's telling you."
---

Lark's analytics dashboard gives you a real-time view of how visitors are interacting with your agent. You can filter analytics by days (7 days or 30 day performance). You can see users by:

- Country
- Browser
- Device
- Languages (any other language than English appears here)

You can also see

- Conversion rates
- Conversation based analytics
  - Depth of conversation
  - Knowledge gaps found in a conversation
  - Unanswered questoins

- Top questions asked (any question asked more than once)
- Top pages where agent interaction happened
- Lead analytics
- Analytics for tools used such as [Estimate builder](/addons/estimate-builder)  (if enabled)
