When a visitor asks a question, Lark searches your knowledge base for relevant content and uses that content to formulate a response. All AI output is provided "**as is**" and "**as available**" without warranty of any kind. You must review AI outputs for accuracy before relying on them.

**Estimates are not binding.** The estimate module generates indicative pricing based on the rules you configure. Estimates are not formal quotes and do not create a binding contract between you and your visitors unless you separately and explicitly make them so.

**You are responsible for your agent.** You decide what knowledge your agent uses, what integrations are connected, and what it says to your visitors. You must:

- review and test your agent's responses before deploying it publicly

- tell your visitors when they are interacting with an AI, where required by law
- not deploy the agent for high-risk uses such as medical diagnosis, legal advice, financial planning, safety-critical decisions, or any use where an error could seriously harm a person

**AI training.** We do not use your content, your visitors' conversations, or any data processed through your Widget to train, fine-tune, or improve AI foundation models — ours or any third party's.
