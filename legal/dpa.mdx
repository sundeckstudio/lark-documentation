**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Lark Terms of Service between **Sundeck Studio FZE LLC** ("Processor", "we", "us") and the customer entity that has agreed to those terms ("Controller", "you").

Where you process personal data of your end users or leads through Lark, this DPA governs how we handle that data on your behalf.

## Sub-processors

We use these sub-processors to run Lark:

| Sub-processor | What it does for Lark |
| --- | --- |
| Anthropic | Generates the agent's AI responses |
| Voyage | Turns text into embeddings so the agent can match questions to a customer's content |
| Supabase | Database storage and authentication |
| Vercel | Hosting, and working out a visitor's country from their network address |
| Stripe | Payments and billing for customer accounts |
| Resend | Sends account and notification emails |
| Composio | Connects the tools a customer chooses to link to Lark |
| Upstash | Rate limiting |

Personal data we pass to a sub-processor is governed by that sub-processor's own agreements, and our responsibility for it ends at transmission under standard contractual clauses.

Integrations or tools you connect yourself (such as but not limited to: Salesforce, Zendesk, Brevo and Mailchimp) are not our sub-processors. Data sent to them is your data and Lark acts as a conduit.

## Conversation data and retention

Lark stores the text of conversations between a customer's website visitors and the customer's agent, and a short summary of a conversation when a visitor gives their contact details. We store this on your behalf and only to provide the Service to you.

We keep conversation text for the following periods, counted from when the conversation takes place:

| Plan | Conversation text kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Free trial (preview conversations) | 14 days |
| Starter | 30 days |
| Pro | 1 year |
| Enterprise | 1 year unless agreed otherwise in writing |

We delete conversation text automatically when its period ends. Deleting a conversation does not delete a lead created from it. You control how long leads and conversation summaries are kept and can delete them at any time. You can also delete a conversation, or all conversations for one visitor, at any time, which you may use to respond to a request from a visitor.

If you connect a tool to receive leads, Lark sends it a copy of the visitor's details and the conversation summary. That copy is outside Lark's control, and deleting a conversation in Lark does not delete it.

When your agreement with us ends, we delete your conversation data within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep it.

## 1. Definitions

**Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed through the Service.
