**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") supplements the Lark Terms of Service (the "**Agreement**") between **Sundeck Studio FZE LLC**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates ("**Lark**", "**we**", "**us**") and the customer entity that has agreed to the Agreement ("**Customer**", "**you**").

By accepting the Agreement, Customer enters into this DPA on behalf of itself and, to the extent required under applicable Data Protection Laws, in the name and on behalf of its Affiliates. Terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the voting securities or equivalent interests.

**"Authorized Sub-Processor"** means a third party engaged by Lark that has a need to access or process Customer Personal Data in order for Lark to perform the Services, as listed in [Exhibit B](#exhibit-b-authorized-sub-processors) or subsequently authorized under Section 5.

**"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.

**"Customer Personal Data"** means Personal Data processed by Lark on Customer's behalf in providing the Services, including Personal Data that Customer processes on behalf of its own end clients.

**"Data Protection Laws"** means all applicable laws and regulations in any relevant jurisdiction relating to the processing of Personal Data or privacy, including without limitation: (i) the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**EU GDPR**"); (ii) the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 ("**UK GDPR**"); (iii) the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection ("**UAE PDPL**") and its executive regulations; (iv) the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 ("**NZ Privacy Act**"); (v) the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) ("**Australian Privacy Act**") and the Australian Privacy Principles; (vi) the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act ("**CCPA/CPRA**"); and (vii) any other applicable national, state or territorial data protection and privacy laws, in each case as amended, supplemented or replaced from time to time.

**"Data Subject"** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

**"EU SCCs"** means the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission in Commission Decision 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.

**"Lark Account Data"** means Personal Data that relates to Lark's relationship with Customer, including names and contact information of Customer's authorized users, and billing contact information.

**"Lark Usage Data"** means Service usage data collected and processed by Lark in connection with providing the Services, including activity logs, analytics, and performance data used to optimize and maintain the Services. Lark Usage Data excludes Customer Personal Data.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under the applicable Data Protection Laws.

**"Personal Data Breach"** means a confirmed breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed by Lark.

**"Processor"** means the entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller.

**"Services"** has the meaning given in the Agreement.

**"Standard Contractual Clauses"** means the EU SCCs and the UK SCCs (as defined in Section 7.3).

**"Supervisory Authority"** means the competent regulatory authority with jurisdiction over the processing of Personal Data under the applicable Data Protection Laws.

## 2. Relationship of the Parties

**2.1 Roles.** The parties acknowledge and agree that:

- With respect to Customer Personal Data processed by Lark on Customer's behalf in providing the Services, **Customer is the Controller and Lark is the Processor**.
- Where Customer itself acts as a Processor on behalf of its own clients, **Lark acts as Customer's sub-processor**. Customer shall ensure it has obtained all necessary authorizations from the relevant Controller to appoint Lark and to provide instructions on that Controller's behalf.
- With respect to Lark Account Data and Lark Usage Data, **Lark acts as an independent Controller** in accordance with Section 10.

**2.2 Customer's Responsibilities.** Customer shall, in its use of the Services, at all times process Personal Data and provide instructions for the processing of Personal Data in compliance with Data Protection Laws. Customer is solely responsible for the accuracy, quality, and legality of (a) the Customer Personal Data provided to Lark; (b) the means by which Customer acquired that Personal Data; and (c) the instructions Customer provides to Lark. Customer shall not provide or make available to Lark any Customer Personal Data in violation of the Agreement or otherwise inappropriate for the nature of the Services.

**2.3 Lark's Processing Obligations.** Lark shall process Customer Personal Data only:

- for the purposes set out in the Agreement and [Exhibit A](#exhibit-a-details-of-processing);
- in accordance with Customer's documented instructions, as set out in this DPA; and
- as required by applicable law, in which case Lark shall, unless prohibited by law, inform Customer before processing.

Customer instructs Lark to process Customer Personal Data as necessary to provide the Services and in accordance with this DPA.

## 3. Data Deletion and Return

**3.1 Deletion on Termination.** Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, or upon Customer's written request, Lark shall delete all Customer Personal Data from its production systems within **fifteen (15) days**. Customer may request a certification of deletion.

**3.2 Backups.** Encrypted backups are retained in line with Lark's standard backup rotation cycle and expire no later than thirty (30) days after the deletion date under Section 3.1, after which no copy of Customer Personal Data remains in Lark's systems.

**3.3 Sub-Processor Deletion.** Lark shall require its Authorized Sub-Processors to delete Customer Personal Data within the same timeframes.

**3.4 Legal Retention.** Lark and its Authorized Sub-Processors may retain Customer Personal Data beyond those periods only to the extent and for the period required by applicable law. Any such retained data shall be isolated, protected in accordance with this DPA, processed only to meet that legal requirement, and deleted when the legal requirement ends.

**3.5 Account Data.** Lark Account Data and Lark Usage Data processed by Lark as an independent Controller under Section 10 are subject to Lark's Privacy Policy and are not governed by this Section.

## 5. Authorized Sub-Processors

**5.1 General Authorization.** Customer provides general written authorization for Lark to engage the Authorized Sub-Processors listed in [Exhibit B](#exhibit-b-authorized-sub-processors) and to engage additional sub-processors as necessary to perform the Services.

**5.2 Notification of Changes.** At least ten (10) days before enabling a new third party (not already listed in Exhibit B) to access or process Customer Personal Data, Lark will notify Customer by updating Exhibit B and sending notice to Customer's registered email address. Customer may subscribe to notifications of changes to Exhibit B via the mechanism Lark makes available.

**5.3 Objection.** Customer may object to the engagement of a new sub-processor by notifying Lark in writing within ten (10) days of receipt of notice, provided the objection is based on reasonable data protection grounds. If Lark cannot provide a commercially reasonable alternative within a reasonable period, Customer may terminate the affected portion of the Services by providing written notice. Termination does not relieve Customer of fees owed. If Customer does not object within ten (10) days, the new sub-processor is deemed authorized.

**5.4 Sub-Processor Agreements.** Lark shall enter into a written agreement with each Authorized Sub-Processor imposing data protection obligations substantially equivalent to those imposed on Lark under this DPA. Lark remains liable to Customer for the Authorized Sub-Processor's performance of those obligations.

**5.5 SCCs.** Where Lark and Customer have entered into the EU SCCs or UK SCCs, the authorizations under this Section constitute Customer's prior written consent for Lark to engage sub-processors as required under those clauses.

**9.1 Records.** Lark shall maintain records sufficient to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA for a period of three (3) years after termination of the Agreement.

**9.2 Audit Rights.** Upon Customer's written request, Lark shall, no more than once per calendar year and subject to reasonable advance notice and confidentiality obligations: (a) provide copies of relevant certifications or audit reports demonstrating compliance with applicable data protection standards; or (b) if the foregoing is not reasonably sufficient under applicable Data Protection Laws, permit Customer's independent third-party auditor to conduct an audit of Lark's data security infrastructure and procedures relevant to Customer Personal Data. **9.3 DPIAs.** Lark shall, upon reasonable request and taking into account the information available to it, provide Customer with reasonable cooperation and assistance to conduct data protection impact assessments and consultations with Supervisory Authorities as required under applicable Data Protection Laws.

**9.4 Unlawful Instructions.** Lark shall promptly notify Customer if, in Lark's reasonable opinion, an instruction from Customer infringes applicable Data Protection Laws.

## 10. Breach Notification

**10.1 Notification.** In the event of a confirmed Personal Data Breach, Lark shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within **seventy-two (72) hours** of becoming aware of the breach (to the extent this is feasible), to the extent the breach relates to Customer Personal Data. Notification shall be provided to Customer's registered email address or via the Service dashboard.

**10.2 Breach Notice Contents.** Lark's initial notification shall include, to the extent then known: (a) a description of the nature of the breach including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and Customer Personal Data records concerned; (b) the name and contact details of the data protection contact; (c) a description of the likely consequences of the breach; and (d) a description of the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Lark shall provide Customer with timely cooperation and assistance as reasonably necessary for Customer to comply with its obligations to notify Supervisory Authorities and affected Data Subjects under applicable Data Protection Laws. Notification or assistance by Lark under this Section does not constitute an acknowledgement of fault or liability.

## Exhibit A — Details of Processing

**Nature and Purpose of Processing**

Lark processes Customer Personal Data as necessary to provide the Services under the Agreement, including: operating Customer's Lark AI agent, processing visitor conversations and support interactions, retrieving information from Customer-provided knowledge sources using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), generating AI responses, and processing contact details, form submissions, and data from integrations configured by Customer.

The nature of processing includes: receiving and recording data; storing, organizing and structuring data; retrieving and analyzing data to respond to visitor queries and provide conversation analytics to Customer; protecting data through access controls, encryption and security testing; deleting data in accordance with Section 3; and sharing data with Authorized Sub-Processors as permitted by this DPA.

**Duration of Processing**

Lark processes Customer Personal Data for the duration of the Agreement, subject to Section 3. Customer Personal Data is deleted from production systems within fifteen (15) days of a written deletion request or termination of the Agreement, with encrypted backups expiring no later than thirty (30) days thereafter.

**Categories of Data Subjects**

- Visitors and end users who interact with Customer's Lark AI agent embedded on Customer's website or digital properties
- Customer's employees, contractors, and authorized users of the Lark dashboard
- Other individuals whose Personal Data Customer or its end users provide through the Services
- Where Customer acts as a Processor, the corresponding individuals of Customer's own clients

**Categories of Personal Data**

Depending on the features used and information provided, Lark may process:

- Visitor interaction data: messages, prompts, AI-generated responses, and conversation transcripts
- Contact information: names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and other details submitted through the agent or lead capture forms
- Analytics data: visitor country (derived from Vercel's `x-vercel-ip-country` header — **IP addresses are never stored**), page URL (path only, maximum 2,000 characters), browser name (parsed from User-Agent), device type (Mobile, Tablet or Desktop), and language (derived from visitor's first message)
- Marketing consent: consent status, consent wording shown, and timestamp of consent (processed pursuant to `lib/consent.ts`)
- Session and conversation identifiers, interaction metadata, and timestamps
- Customer-provided knowledge base content, to the extent it contains Personal Data
- Data from integrations configured by Customer (such as CRM or helpdesk data)

**Sensitive Data**

Customers are prohibited from uploading or transmitting sensitive personal data or special categories of data (as defined under applicable Data Protection Laws) through the Services without first obtaining Lark's written agreement.

**EU/UK SCCs — Party Details**

_Data Exporter:_ Customer, as described in the Agreement. Role: Controller (or Processor where Customer acts as a Processor on behalf of its own clients).

_Data Importer:_ Sundeck Studio FZE LLC, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah, UAE; [privacy@heylark.io](mailto:privacy@heylark.io). Role: Processor (or sub-processor where Customer acts as a Processor).

## Exhibit C — Technical and Organizational Security Measures

The following measures are implemented by Lark in connection with the processing of Customer Personal Data.

**Encryption**

All Customer Personal Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption provided by Lark's hosting infrastructure (Supabase and Vercel). Encryption keys are managed by those providers.

**Access Controls and Authentication**

Access to systems that process Customer Personal Data requires multi-factor authentication. Production access is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis. Customer Personal Data is isolated at the database level using Supabase Row Level Security (RLS), with a `my_tenant_id()` helper enforcing tenant isolation so that one customer cannot access another's data.

**Data Minimization**

Lark does not store visitor IP addresses. Country data is derived at request time from Vercel's geo-enrichment header and only the two-letter ISO country code is stored. Page URLs are truncated to 2,000 characters. Only the data minimally necessary to provide the Services is retained.

**Security Monitoring and Logging**

Lark monitors access to applications, tools, and infrastructure that process Customer Personal Data. Security events are logged and investigated. Anomalous activity is escalated to the engineering team.

**Sub-Processor Due Diligence**

Lark enters into data processing agreements with each Authorized Sub-Processor containing data protection obligations substantially equivalent to those in this DPA.

**Business Continuity and Recovery**

Lark relies on Supabase's continuous backup capability (point-in-time recovery). Backups are retained in accordance with Lark's data retention schedule and are tested periodically.

**Change Management**

Changes to production infrastructure are managed through a code review and CI/CD pipeline process. Configuration changes require authorization from designated engineering personnel.

**Incident Response**

Lark maintains an incident response process. Confirmed Personal Data Breaches are investigated, contained, and notified to Customer in accordance with Section 10.

**Data Deletion**

Customer Personal Data is deleted from production systems within fifteen (15) days of a written deletion request or termination of the Agreement. Encrypted backups are purged within thirty (30) days of that date, as described in Section 3.
