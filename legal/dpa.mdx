**Last updated: 10 October 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Lark Terms of Service between **Sundeck Studio FZE LLC** ("Processor", "we", "us") and the customer entity that has agreed to those terms ("Controller", "you").

Where you process personal data of your end users or leads through Lark, this DPA governs how we handle that data on your behalf.

## Sub-processors

We use these sub-processors to run Lark:

| Sub-processor | What it does for Lark |
| --- | --- |
| Anthropic | Generates the agent's AI responses |
| Voyage | Turns text into embeddings so the agent can match questions to a customer's content |
| Supabase | Database storage and authentication |
| Vercel | Hosting, and working out a visitor's country from their network address |
| Stripe | Payments and billing for customer accounts |
| Resend | Sends account, notification and summary emails |
| Composio | Connects the tools a customer chooses to link to Lark |
| Upstash | Rate limiting |

Personal data we pass to a sub-processor is governed by that sub-processor's own agreements, and our responsibility for it ends at transmission under standard contractual clauses.

Integrations or tools you connect yourself (such as but not limited to: Salesforce, Zendesk, Brevo and Mailchimp) are not our sub-processors. Data sent to them is your data and Lark acts as a conduit.

## 1. Definitions

**Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed through the Service.
