*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

This policy applies to all Lark-operated domains, including **heylark.io** (our marketing website), **app.heylark.io** (the Lark application), and **docs.heylark.io** (our documentation site).

This Privacy Policy explains how Sundeck Studio FZE LLC ("Lark", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit our website at heylark.io, use the Lark application at app.heylark.io, or interact with a Lark-powered chat agent embedded on a third-party website. It also tells you what rights you have over your information and how to exercise them.

Read Section 3 first if you are unsure whether your request should come to us or to the business whose website you were using.

## 2. What we collect, and when

### Visiting heylark.io

When you browse our marketing website we collect the pages you open and when, the type of browser and device you use, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country level only — we do not store your IP address). We use this to understand how visitors use our site and to improve it.

**Analytics.** We use the following analytics tools on heylark.io to understand traffic patterns, page performance, and visitor behaviour:

- **Google Analytics** — traffic, referral sources, and page-level engagement
- **Microsoft Clarity** — session recordings and heatmaps to understand how visitors interact with the site
- **Self-hosted analytics** — a privacy-focused analytics instance we operate, which does not use cookies and does not share data with third parties
- **Google Tag Manager** — used to load and manage analytics and marketing scripts; it does not collect data itself
- **Vercel Analytics** — used on app.heylark.io for performance monitoring; it does not use cookies and processes only aggregate data

Where required by law, cookies set by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are only placed after you give consent through our cookie notice. Our self-hosted analytics and Vercel Analytics do not require consent as they are cookieless and do not track individuals.

**Advertising.** We may run advertising campaigns and use third-party ad platforms (such as Google Ads) to reach potential customers. These platforms may set cookies or use pixels on heylark.io to measure ad performance and, where you have consented, to show relevant ads on other sites. We do not use ad tracking on app.heylark.io or docs.heylark.io.

**Cookie consent.** A cookie notice is displayed to visitors on heylark.io. Analytics and advertising cookies are only set after you actively accept them. You can withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your cookie preferences through the banner or by clearing your browser cookies. Strictly necessary cookies (session management, security) are set regardless of your choice.

**heylark.io visitors are not Lark customers.** People who visit our marketing website have not signed up for an account. We do not link marketing site analytics data to any Lark customer account.

### Creating an account

When you sign up for Lark we collect your name, work email address, and a password. During onboarding we also collect your organisation name, website, location, company size, preferred agent tone, and reply-length preference. If you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect the billing details needed to invoice you, processed by Stripe on our behalf.

### Using the Lark dashboard

While you use Lark we record your actions in the application — settings you change, knowledge sources you add, integrations you connect, and team members you invite. We use this to provide the service and to send you account-related emails such as security notices, billing confirmations, usage warnings, and optional weekly summaries. You can turn optional notifications off in **Account → Notifications**; security notices cannot be turned off.

### Lark agents embedded on other websites

When a visitor opens a Lark-powered chat agent on a customer's website, we collect and store the following on the customer's behalf:

- **Messages** — the text the visitor sends and the agent's replies.
- **Contact details** — name, email address, or phone number the visitor chooses to share.
- **Country** — derived from the visitor's network address by our hosting provider (Vercel). The two-letter country code is stored; the IP address itself is never stored.
- **Page URL** — the address of the page on which the visitor opened the agent (path only, up to 2,000 characters; query strings are not stored).
- **Browser** — the browser name parsed from the User-Agent header (for example, Chrome or Safari). The raw User-Agent string is not stored.
- **Device type** — Mobile, Tablet, or Desktop, parsed from the User-Agent header.
- **Language** — the language of the visitor's first message, detected from the message text.
- **Marketing consent** — where the agent asks whether a visitor wishes to receive marketing emails, we record the visitor's answer, the exact wording shown, and the timestamp. This is stored separately and is never assumed.

This data is collected on behalf of the customer who deployed the agent, not for Lark's own purposes. For that data, the customer is the controller and Lark is the processor. See Section 3 and our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).

### Estimate submissions

When a visitor completes an estimate request through the Lark estimate module, we record their email address, name, answers to the estimate questions, and the resulting estimate. This is stored on behalf of the customer who deployed the estimate.

### Server logs

Our infrastructure providers maintain logs of requests including timestamps and technical metadata necessary to keep the service available and investigate incidents. These logs do not contain the content of conversations.

## 3. When we are the controller, and when we are the processor

**We are the controller** for the personal data of people who visit heylark.io, create a Lark account, or correspond with us. This policy is the notice for that data, and you can send requests directly to us at [info@heylark.io](mailto:info@heylark.io).

**We are the processor** for personal data that a Lark customer's website visitors share through an embedded agent — including conversation messages, contact details, country, page, browser, device type, language, and marketing consent. The customer who deployed the agent decides what is collected, why, and for how long. We process it under a [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa) on that customer's instructions.

If you used a Lark-powered chat agent on another business's website and want to exercise your rights, your request belongs with that business. If you are unsure who to contact, email us at [info@heylark.io](mailto:info@heylark.io) and we will help direct you.

## 4. Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Creating and running your account, delivering the service, billing, and answering support | Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Sending security and billing notices | Contract performance / Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)) |
| Preventing abuse and understanding aggregate usage | Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), balanced against your rights |
| Optional notifications (usage warnings, weekly summaries) | Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); withdraw at any time in Account → Notifications |
| Accounting, tax, and legal compliance | Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Processing visitor data through embedded agents on behalf of customers | Processing under contract with the controller (GDPR Art. 28) |
| Analytics on heylark.io (understanding site traffic and improving the marketing site) | Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) where analytics are non-identifying aggregates; Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for cookies that track individual behaviour |
| Advertising measurement and targeting on heylark.io | Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); only applied after you accept through the cookie notice |

For visitors interacting with an embedded agent, the lawful basis is determined by the customer who deployed it, not by Lark.

Under the **UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**, we process personal data on the grounds of contractual necessity, legitimate interests, and consent as applicable. Under the **New Zealand Privacy Act 2020** and **Australian Privacy Act 1988**, we collect only information necessary for a lawful purpose and handle it in accordance with the applicable privacy principles. For **California residents**, we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined under the CCPA.

## 8. Automated decision-making

The Lark estimate module produces pricing outputs automatically based on the rules configured by the business deploying the agent. These estimates are generated without human intervention. They are **not binding** — they are indicative only and do not constitute a formal quote or contractual offer. No decision based solely on automated processing produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

If you would like a human to review an estimate or any AI-generated output, you can contact the business that deployed the agent directly. If you are unsure how to reach them, email [info@heylark.io](mailto:info@heylark.io) and we will help direct your request.

## 9. AI and your data

We do not train AI models on your data. The content you upload to your knowledge base, the conversations your visitors have, and the contact details they share are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any AI model — ours or a third party's — except with your separate, explicit, opt-in consent.

We may derive anonymised, aggregated industry-level signals from usage patterns across the platform — for example, identifying common question topics within a category of business — to improve default configurations for new accounts in that industry. These signals are statistical in nature; no individual customer's content, conversations, or personal data is identifiable in or extractable from them.

Anthropic and Voyage AI are contractually prohibited from training on data we send them.

Lark uses a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture. Every answer is grounded in content you have provided in your knowledge base. If the answer is not in your knowledge base, the agent says so rather than inventing a response.

## 10. Security

We encrypt personal data in transit using TLS and at rest. Access within Lark is enforced at the database level using Row Level Security, and team access to customer data is restricted to what is necessary to operate the service or respond to a support request.

If a breach occurs that is likely to present a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and notify affected individuals without undue delay where the law requires it.

## 11. Your rights

Where Lark is the controller of your personal data, you have the following rights. The rights available to you depend on where you are located.

| Right | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | Obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you |
| **Rectification** | Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete |
| **Erasure** | Request deletion where no overriding obligation requires us to keep it |
| **Restriction** | Ask us to pause processing while a dispute is open |
| **Portability** | Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests |
| **Withdraw consent** | Stop processing that rests on your consent at any time |

Where we rely on legitimate interests as the legal basis for processing, you may request details of our balancing assessment by emailing [info@heylark.io](mailto:info@heylark.io).

To make a request, email [info@heylark.io](mailto:info@heylark.io) from the address associated with your account. We respond within one calendar month. We may ask you to verify your identity first. There is no charge to exercise these rights.

**California residents** may request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected and the purposes for which it is used, and may opt out of any sale or sharing. Lark does not sell or share personal information as defined under the CCPA.
