## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial. The trial has the same features and limits as Starter, except for conversations: you get 20 live conversations in total during the trial. You can build your agent, add knowledge and put it on your own website to test it with real visitors. Once the 20 conversations are used, the agent asks visitors to leave their email instead of answering. Try agent in your dashboard has its own small limit of 40 messages in any 24 hours and does not use up your 20.

If you have not chosen a plan when the 14 days end, your account is paused and your agent goes offline. Your work is kept, and everything comes back when you choose a plan. Trials cannot be extended.

## Enterprise

For larger teams that need their own LLM key and custom limits. Includes custom SLAs, single sign-on and audit log, a custom domain for the agent, data retention settings and a custom data processing agreement. Pricing is agreed with us, so get in touch at [hello@trylark.io](mailto:hello@trylark.io).

## How billing and usage limits work

- Yearly billing gives you **two months free** (\$290 per year for Starter, \$790 per year for Pro).
- Your conversation allowance resets each calendar month and does not roll over.
- When you reach your allowance the agent asks visitors to leave their email so you can reply, nothing else is charged. We don't charge overage billing. If you would like to buy extra usage limits, send us an email.
- Upgrades apply immediately with proration. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
- Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
