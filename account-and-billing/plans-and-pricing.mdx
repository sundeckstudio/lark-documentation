## Enterprise

For larger teams that need their own LLM key and custom limits. Includes custom SLAs, single sign-on and audit log, a custom domain for the agent, data retention settings and a custom data processing agreement. Pricing is agreed with us, so get in touch at [hello@trylark.io](mailto:hello@trylark.io).

## How billing works

- Starter is \$29 per month and Pro is \$79 per month.
- Yearly billing is ten times the monthly price, which gives you two months free (\$290 per year for Starter, \$790 per year for Pro).
- Your conversation allowance resets each calendar month and does not roll over.
- Each plan has a hard spend cap. When you reach your allowance the agent asks visitors to leave their email so you can reply, and nothing more is charged. There is no overage billing.
- Upgrades apply immediately with proration. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
- Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
