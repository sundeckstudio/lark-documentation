## Lark plans

All plans include the core AI agent, the estimate module, lead capture, and the rules module.

### Starter — $29/month

- 300 conversations per month
- 1 organisation
- Up to 3 integrations
- Lark branding on the agent

### Pro — $79/month

- Unlimited conversations (fair use)
- Up to 3 organisations
- All integrations
- White-label — remove Lark branding
- Priority support

### Enterprise

For larger teams, higher volumes, or custom requirements. Contact us at hello@trylark.io.
