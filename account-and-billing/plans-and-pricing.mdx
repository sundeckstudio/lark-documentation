## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day trial. During the trial you can build your agent, add knowledge, set up your Estimate builder and try the agent inside your dashboard. Putting the agent live on your website needs a paid plan.

## Starter: \$29 per month

- 300 conversations per month
- Up to 40 messages per conversation
- 5 knowledge files, 2 MB each, and 500,000 characters of knowledge
- 1 organisation
- Lark AI agent with the Estimate builder addon
- Brevo, Mailchimp and Zendesk integrations
- Agent learning
- Lead capture, email notifications and CSV exports
- Email support
- Powered by Lark branding

## Pro: \$79 per month

Everything in Starter, plus:

- 1,000 conversations per month
- 20 knowledge files, 5 MB each, and 2,000,000 characters of knowledge
- Up to 3 organisations, with more available as an add-on
- All addons
- All integrations, including Salesforce
- Create and read calendar events
- Priority email support
- Remove Lark branding from the agent and emails

## Enterprise

For larger teams that need their own LLM key and custom limits. Includes custom SLAs, single sign-on and audit log, a custom domain for the agent, data retention settings and a custom data processing agreement. Pricing is agreed with us, so get in touch at [hello@trylark.io](mailto:hello@trylark.io).

## How billing works

- Yearly billing is ten times the monthly price, which gives you two months free ($$290 per year for Starter, $$790 per year for Pro).
- Your conversation allowance resets each calendar month and does not roll over.
- Each plan has a hard spend cap. When you reach your allowance the agent asks visitors to leave their email so you can reply, and nothing more is charged. There is no overage billing.
- Upgrades apply immediately with proration. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
- Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
