---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "Upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan."
---

Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Billing** to see your plan and its limits.

## Changing plans

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancelling

Cancel from the same page. Your plan ends at the end of the billing period, and you can resume before then.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails you have a 3-day grace period. After that your agent stops answering with "This agent isn't available right now" until billing is fixed.

## Refunds

Payments are not refundable. Use the 14-day free trial to try Lark before you pay. See the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service).
