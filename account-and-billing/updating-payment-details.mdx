---
title: "Updating payment details"
description: "Change the card or company details on your invoices."
---

Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Billing and usage**. Use **Replace payment method** to change your card, and edit your company name, address and tax ID under Billing details.

The tax ID label changes with your country, for example VAT, ABN, GST or TRN, and is shown as Tax ID elsewhere.
