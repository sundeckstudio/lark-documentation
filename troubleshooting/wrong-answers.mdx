---
title: "My agent is giving wrong or outdated answers"
description: "How to fix an agent that is providing inaccurate, stale, or unhelpful responses."
icon: "circle-xmark"
---

Lark's agent answers based on the content in your knowledge base. If it is giving inaccurate or stale responses, the most common cause is that the knowledge base hasn't been updated.

## 1. Review and refresh your knowledge base

In your Lark dashboard, go to Knowledge base. Edit outdated text snippets, replace old PDFs, and re-crawl any URLs that have changed.

## 2. Check your rules

A rule that is too broad can cause the agent to deflect questions it should answer. Review and tighten any that may be causing the issue.

## 3. Test with specific questions

Use the preview mode in your dashboard to ask the exact questions visitors are asking. This helps pinpoint whether the issue is missing content or a rules conflict.
