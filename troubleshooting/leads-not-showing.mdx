---
title: "Leads are not showing in my dashboard"
description: "Diagnose why leads captured by your Lark agent are not appearing in the Leads section."
---

If your agent is running but captured leads are not appearing in the Leads section of your dashboard, work through the steps below.

## 1. Check the lead capture rule is active

In your Lark dashboard, go to Rules and confirm you have a lead capture rule toggled on.

## 2. Confirm the agent is live on your site

Leads are only captured from real visitor conversations, not from preview mode. Make sure the embed script is live on your site.

## 3. Check your integration

If you are expecting leads in a connected CRM rather than Lark's Leads list, verify the integration is still connected under Organisation settings → Integrations.
