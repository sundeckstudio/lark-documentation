Squarespace lets you inject custom code into every page from a single place in your settings.

## Before you start

- Your Lark agent is live and your organisation domain is saved in Organisation settings.
- You have a Squarespace Business plan or above. Code injection is not available on Personal plans.

## Get your embed script

In your Lark dashboard open **Add to site** and copy the floating button script.

## Add the script to Squarespace

In your Squarespace dashboard go to **Settings → Advanced → Code Injection**. Paste your Lark script into the **Footer** field and save. The agent will appear on every page of your site after your next publish.

## Inline embed

To show the agent on a specific page, open that page in the editor, add a **Code block**, paste the inline embed code from **Add to site**, and save.

<Note>
  Make sure the domain in Organisation settings matches your live Squarespace domain, not the `.squarespace.com` preview URL.
</Note>
