Framer supports custom code injection at the site level, which puts Lark on every published page with one paste.

## Before you start

- Your Lark agent is live and your organisation domain is saved in Organisation settings.
- You have a Framer site on a paid plan with a connected custom domain.

## Get your embed script

In your Lark dashboard open **Add to site** and copy the floating button script.

## Add the script to Framer

In the Framer editor open **Site Settings** from the top menu. Go to the **General** tab and scroll to **Custom Code**. Paste your Lark script into the **End of `<body>`** field and save. Publish your site and the agent will appear on every page.

## Inline embed

To show the agent inside a specific page, add an **Embed** component from the Insert menu, paste the inline embed code from **Add to site** into the component's HTML field, and resize it to fit your layout.

<Note>
  Make sure the domain in Organisation settings matches your connected Framer domain, not the `.framer.app` preview URL.
</Note>
